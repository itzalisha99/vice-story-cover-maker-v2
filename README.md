Vice Story — a GTA 6-style cover art maker with three different photo moments (sunset street, neon night, beach heist) across three layouts, four colour grades and three post sizes.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — pick a photo for each of the three panels (or use the built-in sample moments), drag to crop, scroll to zoom, write the caption and chapter tags, pick a layout, grade and post size (1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080), and export an exact-size PNG. Works fully offline; photos never leave your machine. Original art only — no Rockstar logo, artwork, character or font.
